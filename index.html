The invitation

Twenty-five years, and the families who made every one of them possible.

An evening of wine, cheese and conversation — a chance to welcome new families, reconnect with familiar faces, and strengthen the connections that hold the PBIS community together.

There are no speeches beyond a short welcome, nothing to sign, and no fundraising table. Just the courtyard by the football field, a long table, and the people whose names your child says at dinner.

Come with a partner or come on your own. You will know someone within ten minutes.

Thank you for being part of our journey.

The evening

6:00 PM

Arrive

The field-side courtyard opens as the light goes. A glass is in your hand before your bag is off your shoulder.

6:20 PM

Welcome

A few words from the Head to mark twenty-five years, and that is the whole of the formal programme.

6:40 PM

Wine

Four bottles, poured and introduced — two white, two red, with sparkling grape and iced tea alongside for anyone not drinking.

7:10 PM

Cheese

A long board of four, from fresh goat to a proper blue, with grapes, walnuts, honey and warm bread.

7:40 PM

Conversation

The part that matters. The courtyard lights come down at eight, and nobody is hurried before then.
